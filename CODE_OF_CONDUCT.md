# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Keep discussions focused on the plugin and its users.
- Respect privacy: do not post tokens, cookies, private session content, or
  another person's logs without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior in issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Encouraging others to break the UI-isolation or security boundaries described
  in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants.

Report incidents privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile:
<https://github.com/HalfDone03>

Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.
